# Self-review of the Ecosphere Comment (manuscript-review pass)

Applied the manuscript-review modules to our own draft. Type: Commentary/Comment. Recommendation
if reviewed: Minor-to-Major revision of a strong, novel, well-supported contribution. Below are the
issues found and how each was handled in the revised draft.

## Major (defensibility) issues addressed
1. **AUC table could be turned against us.** Hagan's LiDAR reaches AUC 0.966 even for OG; a reader
   could cite Table 1 as evidence the method is accurate. Fixed by stating explicitly that
   discrimination on labelled plots is necessary but is achieved by all three predictor sets and so
   cannot distinguish among the maps they produce; retained emphasis on the authors' own 29% OG
   operating accuracy.
2. **Our maps are also proxies.** Added a clear statement in Section 3 that none of the products is
   ground truth, including ours; the canopy-height map and TreeMap are proxies, TreeMap smooths rare
   classes (its 30 m area is a lower bound, its pixel agreement weak). Reframed the cross-map result
   as demonstrating uncertainty among credible products, not that Hagan is wrong.
3. **"3x big-tree vs old" mixes criteria.** Hedged as illustrative; noted the large-tree threshold is
   not definitional and that age and structure measure different things.
4. **FIA stand age is modeled/uncertain in uneven-aged stands.** Acknowledged in Section 5; leaned on
   the large-tree basal-area structural domain as the complement; noted the two criteria bracket a
   consistent range.
