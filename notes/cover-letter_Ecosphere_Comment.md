# Cover letter: Comment to Ecosphere

Dear Editor,

Please consider the enclosed Comment, "Using LiDAR to quantify, map, and conserve late-successional and old-growth forest in Maine, USA: Comment," for publication in Ecosphere. It responds to Hagan et al. (2026, Ecosphere 17(6):e70670).

Mapping mature and old-growth forest has become a national undertaking since the 2022 federal executive order, and a recurring lesson across that work is that independent, individually credible maps disagree substantially on both how much old forest exists and where it sits. The Hagan et al. classification is a valuable and transparent contribution, and we treat the Maine case as a concrete instance of that general problem rather than as a special one. Working largely from the authors' own archived training data and from independent public data over the same area, we show what a cross-map accuracy assessment and an explicit, design-based uncertainty statement add before a single map anchors parcel-level conservation spending. We reproduce the published classifier, document that its rare old-growth class is detected about 29 percent of the time and was not field-validated, show that equally defensible operationalizations of the same condition place markedly different amounts of forest in markedly different places, and provide design-based Forest Inventory and Analysis estimates with confidence intervals that the map-only accounts omit.

The Comment is constructive in intent. It does not argue against mapping or conserving older forest, but for reporting a method range with its sampling interval, cross-checking any single map against an independent product, and field-verifying candidate ground before high-stakes allocation. All supporting analyses and derived products are openly archived at Zenodo (concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20614496).

We note a competing interest: we are developing an independent, FIA-anchored multi-axis classification of late-successional and old-growth forest, which the Comment references as a companion analysis and which we hold to the same standard we apply here. The work has not been published previously and is not under consideration elsewhere.

Thank you for your consideration.
